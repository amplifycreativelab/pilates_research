Read this first: prices, shipping & rankings

All prices are AUD. Base prices exclude listed customization add-ons. “@” means “at this order quantity”; MOQ means minimum order quantity. Socks are per pair except Soxtown, which lists pieces. Price ranges can cover different variants.

Freight estimates use Australia, postcode 2000. This was the browser default, not your confirmed address. Some selected orders include a promotion capped at A$30. Free shipping on a small sample order does not mean free bulk shipping. Missing freight or customization charges are unknown, not zero. Ask for a fresh quote to your postcode; taxes and other charges may apply.

Rank is within each product category. It reflects cost, customization minimums, relevant specifications and available manufacturing evidence for a small Australian brand that wants to scale. It is a shortlist from screened listings, not an audit of every Alibaba supplier. Company data and dispatch rates are displayed platform / supplier claims; dispatch is not delivery performance. Revenue stated in company profiles is US dollars.

The source report was read and 48 listing cards per category were screened before inspecting these 45 product pages. Mats include different materials that should be quoted separately; balls here are small inflatable Pilates balls, not large Swiss balls or weighted toning balls.

Start with: Sansd / Ugasport / Boga for TPE mats; Changhong / Shengde / Kaitai for balls; Kangyi / Zhongde / Kaihui for socks. Fuli is a budget sock alternative; Boyu offers a small branded pilot.

What to ask before ordering

Request comparable quotes for 100 / 300 / 500 / 1,000 units, separating product, logo setup, printing, packaging, sample credit and freight. Ask Zhongde for 600 pairs as well. Confirm stock versus custom minimums.

  • Mats: fix material, dimensions and thickness; ask for carton size and weight. Test stability, dry/wet grip, compression recovery and rolled storage.
  • Balls: compare 20–22 cm and 25 cm separately; specify weight, logo, inflation straw and spare plug. Check overnight pressure retention and valve durability.
  • Socks: fix crew or strap style, fibre percentages, sizes, silicone coverage and logo method. Test fit and grip after repeated washing.
  • Every supplier: confirm actual factory, capacity for this product, production slots, lead time, sample approval and defect remedy. Requote freight to your actual Australian postcode.
